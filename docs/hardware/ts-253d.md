# Reference hardware: TS-253D with a QM2-2P-244A-A1

Everything here was observed on the project's reference box in October
2026 unless it says otherwise. It is the ground truth for defaults and
tests; other models are expected to differ in counts, not in kind.

## Platform

| Item | Value |
|-|-|
| NAS | QNAP TS-253D, Intel Celeron J4125 (4C, 10 W TDP, T<sub>junction</sub> 105 °C per Intel ark), one 70 mm chassis fan, 2 SATA bays |
| OS | OpenMediaVault 8.5 on Debian 13 (Trixie), Proxmox kernel `7.0.x-pve` (headers: `proxmox-headers-*`) |
| Boot disk | USB SSD (non-removable flag, so it shows up as a SATA `sdX` disk) |
| Internal eMMC | 4 GB (`mmc-…`), holds the original QTS firmware; ignored by the plugin |
| Expansion | QM2-2P-244A-A1 in the PCIe slot: 2 × M.2 NVMe, ASMedia ASM1812 switch (`1b21:1812`), own fan, PIC firmware 1.13 |
| Drivers | qnap8528 1.24 (DKMS), qm2mod 1.1 (DKMS), both via `/etc/modules-load.d` |

## qnap8528 on this box

- **Chip check passes without `skip_hw_check`** (dmesg: `IT8528 EC device
  found successfully`), contrary to the upstream list of ENE-EC models.
- Model detection: `MB=70-0Q05H0201` + backplane `70-1QY570111` →
  `device model is TS-253D` (kernel log only; not in sysfs).
- hwmon `qnap8528`: `fan1_input`, `pwm1`, `temp1_input` (chassis, ≈21–28 °C
  idle), `temp6_input` (backplane, ≈25–32 °C). No junk channels.
- LEDs: `status`, `usb`, `hdd1`, `hdd2`, `panel_brightness`. **hdd1 = bay 1 =
  SATA port 1 (`ata1`), hdd2 = bay 2 = `ata2`** (verified by lighting each
  LED and by reading from one disk only).
- VPD strings are **NUL padded** to 32 bytes; VPD reads cost ~5 ms per byte
  (`qnapd info` took 1.9 s uncached, 0.14 s with the per-boot cache).
- EC firmware `Q04WE018`; CPLD reads `0xff` (not present). BIOS from DMI
  (`Q04WAR09`); DMI product/serial fields are "Default string".
- `ec/power_recovery` was 2 (restore last state): after an unplanned power
  cut the box powered itself back on.

## Buttons

Input device `qnap8528` (`event4` on the reference box): Reset (rear
pinhole) = `BTN_1`, USB Copy (front) = `BTN_2`, chassis open = `BTN_0`
(not tested). Press and release are separate events; a 7 s Reset hold
measured correctly. The power button is ACPI and shuts the box down.

## qm2mod on this card

- `swap=0` (exact model match), 2 slots, 2 temperature sensors (one per
  slot, ≈28–38 °C), I2C adapter number appears in LED names
  (`qm2-<n>:{green,red}:slot{1,2}`).
- **Slot 1 = switch downstream port `02:00.0` = `nvme0`, slot 2 = `02:08.0` =
  `nvme1`** (verified by lighting each LED and by reading from nvme0 only).
- LEDs are all off until something sets them; the PIC drives nothing itself.
- `fan_default` hands the fan to the PIC, which idles it at PWM 64 /
  ≈1260 RPM.

## Measured fan curves

PWM written vs RPM, 15 s per step, idle system:

| PWM | 255 | 192 | 128 | 96 | 80 | 64 | 56 | 48 | 40 | 32 | 16 | 8 |
|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|-|
| Chassis (70 mm) | 3631 | 2804 | 1905 | 1407 | 1191 | 944 | 786 | 663 | 546 | 397 | 342 | stall |
| QM2-2P-244A-A1 | 5860 | 4740 | 3320 | 2440 | 1840 | 1260 | 880 | stall | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- Chassis fan stalls between PWM 16 and 8 and **restarts only at 32**.
- QM2 fan stalls at 48 and barely restarts at 48 (160 RPM).
- qnap8528 stores PWM as a percentage, so read-back drifts (128 → 127,
  56 → 53).
- QM2 RPM comes in 60 RPM steps.

## Noise (listener at the box)

- QM2 fan at 64: not audible over the chassis fan at 48. Audible from 96.
- Chassis around 1300 RPM (PWM ≈86) at idle was acceptable.
- What *was* distracting: the chassis fan jumping with CPU bursts, and the
  QM2 fan at full speed (≈5900 RPM) for a few seconds on every restart.
  Both are fixed (see [fan-control.md](fan-control.md)).

## Temperatures and load seen

- CPU package idles 45–52 °C; bursty background load (ZFS kworkers, an open
  OMV dashboard) spikes single samples to 60–68 °C.
- An open OMV dashboard with many widgets kept two php-fpm workers at ≈80 %
  of a core. Close or navigate away from it when measuring.
- HDDs (WD 12 TB) 33–39 °C, NVMe 40–42 °C, QM2 slot sensors 37–38 °C.

## Disks and pools

- 2 × 12 TB SATA disks in a ZFS mirror; 2 × NVMe on the QM2 in a ZFS mirror.
- OMV spindown configured (APM 1, 10 min). Anything calling `smartctl`
  without `-n standby` keeps them awake (a tempmon setup did).
